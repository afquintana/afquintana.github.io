Privacy Policy for QR Scanner

Last updated: May 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QR Scanner handles information when you use the application. The app is designed to scan QR codes quickly and privately, with the minimum permissions required to provide its core functionality.

1. Information We Collect

QR Scanner does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or address.

The app may process QR code content that you scan, such as text, URLs, Wi-Fi details, contact information, or other data encoded in the QR code. This processing is performed to show you the scan result and to allow you to interact with it.

2. Camera Permission

QR Scanner requires access to your device camera in order to scan QR codes. Camera images are used only for scanning purposes. The app does not use the camera permission to identify you, track you, or collect personal information.

3. Local Processing

QR code scanning is intended to be processed on your device. The content of scanned QR codes is not sent to our servers by the app. If you open a scanned link, the website or external service you open may collect information according to its own privacy policy.

4. Scan History and Saved Data

If the app includes a scan history or saved results feature, that information is stored locally on your device so you can view previous scans. You can delete this information from within the app, if such option is available, or by clearing the app data/uninstalling the app from your device settings.

5. Third-Party Services

QR Scanner may use third-party services provided by Google Play or the Android operating system to distribute, maintain, or improve the app. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

If advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or similar third-party SDKs are added to the app in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe those services and the information they may process.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share scanned QR code content with third parties, except when you choose to open, share, copy, or otherwise use the scanned content through another app or service.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable measures to keep the app safe and to limit data processing to what is necessary for the app to function. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Children’s Privacy

QR Scanner is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

9. Your Choices

  • You can deny camera permission, although QR scanning will not work without it.
  • You can delete locally stored app data through your device settings.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the app listing or the official support channel provided in Google Play.
