Privacy Policy - Big Text

Last updated: May 24, 2026

Big Text respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the Android application and how that information is handled.

Privacy summary: Big Text is designed to work mostly on your device. Text you type is used to show large, readable messages on screen and is not sold by us.

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Big Text Android application.

Privacy contact:
mail@afquintana.com

2. Information the App May Process

Depending on the features enabled in your build of the app, Big Text may process:

  • Text input you provide to display messages in large format.
  • Display preferences, such as text size, style, color, and screen behavior settings.
  • Technical information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, and basic diagnostics.
  • Usage, crash, or analytics data, if analytics or crash reporting tools are integrated.
  • Advertising-related data, such as advertising identifiers and ad interaction events, if ads are enabled.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To render and display your text at large size.
  • To save your selected preferences and improve usability.
  • To diagnose crashes, fix issues, and improve stability.
  • To measure app performance and usage trends (when analytics is enabled).
  • To display and measure advertising performance (when ads are enabled).

4. Local Processing and Storage

Big Text is intended to process core functionality locally on your device. Text you enter and settings you choose may be stored locally so the app can remember your configuration.

5. Third-Party Services

If included in the app, third-party services (for example, Google Play services, Firebase, or AdMob) may process technical, analytics, crash, or advertising data according to their own policies.

You can review Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with service providers when needed to operate, secure, analyze, monetize, or improve the app, or when required by law.

7. Data Retention

Data stored on your device remains until you remove it, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Data handled by third-party providers may be retained according to their retention practices.

8. Children's Privacy

Big Text is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law without parental authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal information in violation of applicable law, contact us so we can review and address the issue.

9. Your Choices

  • You can stop using the app at any time.
  • You can clear local app data from Android settings.
  • You can manage ad personalization settings in your Android device settings, when relevant.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at mail@afquintana.com.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates when this version became effective.
