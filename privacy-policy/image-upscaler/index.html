Privacy Policy for Image Upscaler

Last updated: May 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Image Upscaler handles information when you use the mobile application. If you have any questions, you can contact us at mail@afquintana.com.

Privacy summary: Image Upscaler only accesses images that you select. Image processing is performed to provide the app's upscaling and enhancement features, and we do not sell your personal information.

1. Information We Do Not Request

Image Upscaler does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.

2. Images and Device Storage

The app may ask you to select images from your device so it can upscale, enhance, preview, save, or share the resulting files. Android grants the app access only to the images or folders that you choose through the system picker or permission prompts.

  • The app does not scan all of your device storage.
  • The app does not access images that you have not selected or authorized.
  • Generated images are saved locally on your device or shared only when you choose to do so.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your images or personal information.

3. Image Processing

Image Upscaler uses the images you select only to provide image upscaling, enhancement, preview, saving, and sharing features. Temporary files may be created on your device while the app processes or exports an image. These files are used for app functionality and are not intended to be stored on developer-operated servers.

4. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Image Upscaler may use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand app usage and diagnose crashes. These services may collect technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, crash logs, performance information, and basic usage events.

This information helps us improve reliability and user experience. Analytics and crash reporting data is not used to sell your personal information and does not provide us with the contents of your selected images.

5. Third-Party Services

The app may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics
  • Android system image picker, storage, and sharing features

These services are governed by their own privacy terms. You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Sharing and External Apps

If you choose to share an image, open it with another app, save it to cloud storage, or send it through an external service, that action is controlled by you and is subject to the privacy practices of the selected app or service.

7. Data Retention

Your original and generated images remain on your device or in the location you choose. We do not maintain copies of your images on developer-operated servers. Firebase may retain analytics and crash information according to Google's retention practices.

8. Children's Privacy

Image Upscaler is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, delete, or object to the processing of personal data. Because Image Upscaler does not require accounts, most app data remains under your control on your own device. You can delete generated images, clear app data, revoke permissions, or uninstall the app at any time through your device settings.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the app's privacy practices, contact us at mail@afquintana.com.
