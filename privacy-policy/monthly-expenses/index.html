Privacy Policy - Monthly Expenses

Last updated: May 13, 2026

Monthly Expenses respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may handle, how that information is used, how long it may be retained, and how users can request deletion of their data.

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Monthly Expenses Android application.

Privacy contact:
mail@afquintana.com

2. Data We May Collect

Depending on how you use the app, Monthly Expenses may process the following categories of information:

  • Expense records, such as amounts, categories, dates, notes, recurring expense details, and other information you manually enter in the app.
  • Account data, such as your email address, if the app provides sign-in, account, backup, or synchronization features.
  • Device and app information, such as app version, device model, operating system version, and technical identifiers used for diagnostics.
  • Diagnostics and crash data, such as error reports and performance information used to detect issues and improve stability.
  • Analytics data, such as general app interaction information used to understand usage and improve the user experience, where enabled.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information may be used to:

  • Provide the app features you request, including recording, organizing, calculating, and displaying your monthly expenses.
  • Save your preferences and user-entered financial tracking information.
  • Synchronize, back up, or restore information across sessions or devices if those features are available and enabled.
  • Maintain app security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Analyze app performance and improve features, reliability, and user experience.

4. Financial Information

Monthly Expenses is designed for personal expense tracking. The app does not request bank credentials, credit card numbers, or payment account passwords. Any financial details you enter are used to provide the expense tracking features of the app.

5. Data Storage and Service Providers

Some information may be stored locally on your device. If the app includes optional online features such as account sign-in, cloud backup, analytics, or crash reporting, data may be processed using third-party service providers that are necessary to operate, secure, maintain, or improve the app.

These providers may process data on our behalf in accordance with their own terms and security standards.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with service providers when it is necessary to operate, secure, maintain, or improve the app, or when required by law.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the app and its features, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect the app against fraud or abuse.

  • User-entered expense data may remain on your device until you delete it, clear the app data, or uninstall the app.
  • Account or synced data, where applicable, may be kept while your account remains active or until deletion is requested.
  • Analytics, crash information, backups, and technical logs may be retained for a limited period for operational monitoring and app improvement.

8. How to Request Deletion of Your Data

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time, where account or cloud-based features are available. You can also delete locally stored information from within the app, through your device settings, by clearing app data, or by uninstalling the app.

To request deletion, email mail@afquintana.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request - Monthly Expenses and include enough information for us to identify your app account or data, where applicable.

When a valid deletion request is received, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize, as applicable:

  • Your app account information, where applicable.
  • Your expense entries, categories, budgets, recurring expense records, and other user-generated records stored for the app, where applicable and technically possible.
  • Associated diagnostic or support information that can reasonably be linked to you, where applicable.

Some information may be retained when required by law, for legitimate security purposes, for fraud prevention, to resolve disputes, or when retained temporarily in backups or technical logs.

9. Your Choices

You may choose not to provide certain information, but some app features may not work correctly without it. You may also contact us using the email above to ask questions about your data.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect personal data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

11. Children's Privacy

Monthly Expenses is not intended for children unless expressly stated otherwise in the app listing. If you believe personal data has been provided by a child inappropriately, please contact us so we can review and delete it when appropriate.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page.

13. Contact

For any privacy-related question, data request, or deletion request, contact: mail@afquintana.com

This page is intended to be publicly accessible as the privacy policy for the Monthly Expenses application.
