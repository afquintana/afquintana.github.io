Privacy Policy for Unir PDF

Last updated: April 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Unir PDF handles information when you use the mobile application. If you have any questions, you can contact us at mail@afquintana.com.

Privacy summary: Unir PDF processes your PDF files on your device. Your PDF files are not uploaded to our servers, and we do not sell your personal information.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Unir PDF is designed to merge PDF files locally on your device. The app does not require an account, and we do not collect, store, or upload the contents of your PDF files.

  • Your selected PDF files remain on your device.
  • The merged PDF is created locally on your device.
  • We do not access, read, or store the contents of your documents on any server.

2. Files and Device Storage

When you select PDF files, Android grants the app access only to the files you choose. The app uses this access to read the selected PDFs, show previews, remove selected pages if requested, and create the merged PDF. The merged file may be saved to a folder you select or shared using Android's built-in sharing tools.

3. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Unir PDF uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand app usage and diagnose crashes. These services may collect technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, crash logs, performance information, and basic usage events.

This information helps us improve reliability and user experience. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics do not provide us with the contents of your PDF files.

4. Third-Party Services

The app may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics
  • Android system file picker and sharing features

These services are governed by their own privacy terms. You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Advertising

Unir PDF does not currently show advertising. If advertising is added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain which advertising services are used and what data may be processed.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your PDF files with third parties. Technical analytics and crash information may be processed by Google Firebase as described above.

7. Data Retention

The app does not store your PDF files on our servers. Any temporary files created during processing are stored locally on your device. Firebase may retain analytics and crash information according to Google's retention practices.

8. Children's Privacy

Unir PDF is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, delete, or object to the processing of personal data. Because we do not collect your PDF files or require accounts, most app data remains under your control on your own device. You can clear app data or uninstall the app at any time through your device settings.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the app's privacy practices, contact us at mail@afquintana.com.
