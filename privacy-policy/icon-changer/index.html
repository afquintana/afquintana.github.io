Privacy Policy - Icon Changer

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Icon Changer respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may access, how that information is used, and the choices you have when using the Android application.

Privacy summary: Icon Changer uses the apps, icons, images, and labels you choose only to create custom shortcuts on your device. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Icon Changer Android application.

Privacy contact:
mail@afquintana.com

2. Information the App May Access

Depending on the features you use, Icon Changer may access or process the following information:

  • Installed app information, such as app names, package names, and icons, so you can choose which app shortcut to customize.
  • Images or icons you select from your device, icon packs, or supported Android pickers to create a custom shortcut icon.
  • Shortcut labels and preferences that you enter or configure in the app.
  • Technical and device information, such as app version, device model, operating system version, language, and diagnostics needed to operate and improve the app.
  • Analytics and crash data, such as general usage events, crash logs, and performance information, if analytics or crash reporting services are enabled.
  • Advertising-related data, such as advertising identifiers or ad interaction data, if the app displays advertising through third-party advertising services.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Display apps installed on your device so you can select one for shortcut customization.
  • Create, preview, save, and update custom shortcut icons and labels.
  • Store your selected preferences locally on your device.
  • Maintain app security, troubleshoot issues, and improve app stability and user experience.
  • Display advertising and measure ad performance, if advertising is enabled in the app.

4. Device Storage, Images, and Shortcuts

Icon Changer may ask you to select images or icons from your device. Android grants access only to the items or folders you choose through system permission prompts or pickers. Generated shortcuts and related preferences are intended to remain on your device unless you choose to share content with another app or service.

  • The app does not access images that you have not selected or authorized.
  • The app uses selected images and icons to provide shortcut creation and customization features.
  • You can remove generated shortcuts from your launcher or clear app data from Android settings.

5. Data Storage and Service Providers

Data may be stored locally on your device and may also be processed using third-party services that help us operate, secure, analyze, monetize, or improve the app.

These services may include Google services such as Google Play services, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or Google AdMob, if they are integrated into the app. These providers process data according to their own privacy policies, terms, and security standards.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with service providers when it is necessary to operate, secure, maintain, analyze, monetize, or improve the app, or when required by law.

7. Advertising

If Icon Changer displays ads, third-party advertising providers may collect or receive information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, and ad interaction data to provide, limit, personalize, measure, or improve advertising, depending on your device settings, consent choices, and applicable law.

You can manage ad personalization and advertising identifier settings from your Android device settings.

8. Data Retention

Information saved locally in the app may remain on your device until you delete it, clear the app data, remove generated shortcuts, or uninstall the app. Analytics, crash, advertising, or technical information may be retained for a limited period by the relevant service providers as part of operational monitoring, reporting, security, monetization, and app improvement.

9. How to Request Deletion of Your Data

You may request deletion of personal data associated with your use of Icon Changer at any time.

To request deletion, email mail@afquintana.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request - Icon Changer and include enough information for us to identify the data related to your request.

Some information may be retained when required by law, for legitimate security purposes, for fraud prevention, to resolve disputes, or when retained temporarily in backups or technical logs.

10. Your Choices

You may choose not to grant certain permissions, but some app features may not work correctly without them. You can revoke permissions, delete locally stored app data, remove generated shortcuts, or uninstall the app at any time through your device settings.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect personal data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

12. Children's Privacy

Icon Changer is not intended for children unless expressly stated otherwise in the app listing. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it when appropriate.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page.

14. Contact

For any privacy-related question, data request, or deletion request, contact: mail@afquintana.com
