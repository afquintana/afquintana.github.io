Privacy Policy - Buy Cheaper

Last updated: April 29, 2026

Buy Cheaper respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app may collect, how that data is used, how long it may be retained, and how users can request deletion of their personal data.

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Buy Cheaper Android application.

Privacy contact:

mail@afquintana.com

2. Data We May Collect

Depending on how you use the app, Buy Cheaper may collect or process the following categories of data:

  • Product, search, or comparison information that you enter in the app, such as product names, product links, stores, prices, notes, or items you choose to compare.
  • App preferences and locally saved information, such as selected products, favorite items, recent searches, preferred currency, language, or display settings.
  • Technical and device information needed to operate the app, such as app version, device model, operating system version, language, and general diagnostic information.
  • Diagnostics and crash data, such as technical information needed to detect errors, fix bugs, and improve app stability.
  • Analytics data, such as general app interaction information used to understand usage and improve the user experience.
  • Advertising-related data, such as advertising identifiers or ad interaction data, if the app displays advertising through third-party advertising services.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data may be used to:

  • Provide the app's product search, price comparison, tracking, saving, and sharing features.
  • Store and display products, prices, notes, or comparison information that you add or select in the app.
  • Retrieve or display product, price, availability, or store information from online services, where applicable.
  • Remember your preferences and improve the usability of the app.
  • Maintain app security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Analyze app performance and improve features, reliability, and user experience.
  • Display advertising and measure ad performance, if advertising is enabled in the app.

4. Data Storage and Service Providers

Data may be stored locally on your device and may also be processed using third-party services that are necessary to operate, secure, maintain, analyze, monetize, or improve the app.

These services may include Google services such as Google Play services, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or Google AdMob, if they are integrated into the app. The app may also interact with external websites, stores, APIs, or online services to obtain product, price, or availability information.

These providers may process data on our behalf or under their own privacy policies, terms, and security standards.

5. External Websites and Third-Party Content

Buy Cheaper may show or open links to external websites, online stores, or third-party content. When you visit or interact with those external services, their own privacy policies and terms apply. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or services.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share data with service providers when it is necessary to operate, secure, maintain, analyze, monetize, or improve the app, or when required by law.

7. Advertising

If Buy Cheaper displays ads, third-party advertising providers may collect or receive information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, and ad interaction data in order to provide, limit, personalize, measure, or improve advertising, depending on your device settings, consent choices, and applicable law.

You can manage ad personalization and advertising identifier settings from your Android device settings.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the app and its features, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, maintain security, prevent fraud or abuse, and improve the app.

  • Information saved locally in the app may remain on your device until you delete it, clear the app data, or uninstall the app.
  • Analytics, crash, advertising, or technical information may be retained for a limited period by the relevant service providers as part of operational monitoring, reporting, security, monetization, and app improvement.
  • Backups or technical logs may remain for a limited period before they are automatically overwritten or deleted.

9. How to Request Deletion of Your Data

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time.

To request deletion, email mail@afquintana.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request - Buy Cheaper and include enough information for us to identify the data related to your request.

When a valid deletion request is received, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize, as applicable:

  • Personal information associated with your request, where applicable.
  • Product, price, comparison, or user-generated records stored by the app or related services, where applicable and technically possible.
  • Associated diagnostic or support information that can reasonably be linked to you, where applicable.

Some information may be retained when required by law, for legitimate security purposes, for fraud prevention, to resolve disputes, or when retained temporarily in backups or technical logs.

10. Your Choices

You may choose not to provide certain information, but some app features may not work correctly without it. You may also delete locally stored app data from your device settings, uninstall the app, or contact us using the email above to ask questions about your data.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect personal data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

12. Children's Privacy

Buy Cheaper is not intended for children unless expressly stated otherwise in the app listing. If you believe personal data has been provided by a child inappropriately, please contact us so we can review and delete it when appropriate.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page.
