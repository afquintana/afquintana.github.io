Privacy Policy for Notify Log

Last updated: May 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Notify Log handles information when you use the app. Notify Log is designed to help you capture and organize notification activity while keeping data processing transparent and limited to what is necessary for core functionality.

1. Information We Collect

Notify Log does not require you to create an account and does not request personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, or home address.

To provide its features, Notify Log may process notification-related information shown on your device, such as app name, notification title, message text, timestamp, and related metadata available through Android notification access.

2. Notification Access Permission

Notify Log requires Notification Access permission so it can read incoming notifications and store log entries for your review. Without this permission, core logging features may not work.

The permission is used only for the app's logging and organization features and not for identity profiling or unrelated tracking.

3. Local Data Storage

Notification logs are stored locally on your device by default so you can browse, filter, and manage your history. Notify Log does not upload notification content to developer-owned servers unless a specific cloud or backup feature is clearly enabled by you in the future.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. Notification content is not shared with third parties, except when you explicitly choose to export, share, or synchronize data using external apps or services.

5. Third-Party Services

Notify Log may rely on platform-level services (such as Google Play and Android system services) for app distribution, updates, and stability. These services may process limited technical information under their own privacy policies.

If analytics, crash reporting, cloud sync, or advertising SDKs are added in a later version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe what data is involved and why.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Logged notifications remain on your device until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall Notify Log. If in-app cleanup tools are available, you can use them to remove specific entries or all history.

7. Security

We apply reasonable safeguards to reduce unauthorized access and limit processing to intended functionality. However, no storage method is completely secure.

8. Children’s Privacy

Notify Log is not directed to children and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your Choices

  • You can disable Notification Access permission at any time in device settings.
  • You can delete stored log data using in-app tools or by clearing app data.
  • You can uninstall Notify Log at any time.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the support channel listed in the app store listing.
