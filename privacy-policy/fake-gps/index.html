Privacy Policy for Fake GPS

Last updated: May 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fake GPS handles information when you use the mobile application. Fake GPS is designed to spoof or simulate the device location for development, testing, quality assurance, and validation of applications that depend on location.

Privacy summary: Fake GPS uses the location permissions needed to simulate locations on your device. The app does not sell personal information, does not create advertising profiles, and does not upload your location to our own servers.

1. Information We Process

Fake GPS may process the following information locally on your device:

  • Location coordinates that you select manually or through the app map.
  • Usage preferences, such as the last simulated location or settings needed to run the app.
  • Basic technical device information required by Android to grant permissions and run services.

2. How We Use Information

The information is used only to:

  • Let you choose a fake or simulated location for testing.
  • Start, stop, and maintain location simulation while the app is active.
  • Remember local settings to improve the user experience.
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve app stability.

3. Android Permissions

Fake GPS may request permissions related to location and background operation:

  • Location: needed to interact with Android location APIs and simulate coordinates.
  • Foreground services or notifications: may be used to keep the simulation status visible.
  • Developer options / mock location app: Android may require you to select Fake GPS as the mock location app for the feature to work properly.

You can revoke permissions at any time from your device settings, although some features will no longer be available.

4. Data Storage and Transmission

Fake GPS is intended to process information on your device. We do not upload the locations that you enter or simulate to our own servers. If the app includes third-party services for technical features, those services may process limited information under their own policies.

5. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party components or services, such as maps, technical analytics, crash reports, or Android/Google Play platform services. These services may collect technical information according to their privacy policies and your device settings.

6. Responsible Use

Fake GPS is provided for legitimate testing, development, and validation. You are responsible for complying with applicable laws, the terms of service of other applications, and any policy that prohibits spoofing location in certain services.

7. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information for commercial purposes. We may only disclose information if necessary to comply with legal obligations, protect rights, or respond to valid requests from competent authorities.

8. Security

We apply reasonable measures to limit data processing to what is necessary for the app to function. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Fake GPS is not directed to children and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us so we can review it.

10. Your Choices

  • You can deny or revoke permissions from your device settings.
  • You can delete the app's local data from Android settings.
  • You can stop using the simulation and uninstall the application at any time.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page and indicate a new “Last updated” date.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer at mail@afquintana.com or through the support channel listed on Google Play.
