Privacy Policy for File Explorer

Last updated: May 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how File Explorer handles information when you use the application. The app is designed to help you browse, organize, and manage files on your device while minimizing data collection and keeping your information under your control.

1. Information We Collect

File Explorer does not require account registration and does not ask for personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or home address.

To provide file management features, the app may access and process file metadata (for example file names, file paths, sizes, and modification dates) and selected file content directly on your device.

2. Storage Permissions

File Explorer may request file and storage access permissions (such as media or all-files access, depending on your Android version) to let you view, copy, move, rename, delete, or share files.

These permissions are used only to provide requested file management actions and are not used to identify you personally.

3. Local Processing

File browsing and file operations are intended to happen locally on your device. Your files are not uploaded to our servers by default.

If you choose to share a file or open it with another app, the selected third-party app or service may process that file according to its own privacy policy.

4. Data Storage

The app may store user preferences locally, such as sorting mode, view settings, favorite folders, and recent locations, to improve usability.

You can clear app data from your device settings or uninstall the app at any time to remove locally stored settings.

5. Third-Party Services

File Explorer may rely on third-party services provided by Android or Google Play for distribution, updates, and app stability.

If analytics, crash reporting, cloud features, or advertising SDKs are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe what data is processed and for what purpose.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share your files with third parties unless you explicitly trigger an action, such as sharing, exporting, or opening a file in another app.

7. Data Security

We apply reasonable measures to protect app functionality and limit data processing to what is needed for core features. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children’s Privacy

File Explorer is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so appropriate action can be taken.

9. Your Choices

  • You can deny storage permissions, although file management features may not work fully.
  • You can clear app data through your device settings.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the app listing or the official support channel provided in Google Play.
