Privacy Policy for QR Generate

Last updated: May 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QR Generate handles information when you use the application. The app is designed to generate QR codes from content that you enter, with the minimum data processing required to provide its core functionality.

1. Information We Collect

QR Generate does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or address.

The app may process the content that you enter in order to generate a QR code. This may include text, URLs, Wi-Fi details, contact information, or any other information you choose to convert into a QR code.

2. QR Code Generation

QR codes are generated from the information that you voluntarily enter into the app. You are responsible for the content you choose to include in a QR code. Avoid entering sensitive information unless you understand that anyone who scans the QR code may be able to read its content.

3. Local Processing

QR code generation is intended to be processed on your device. The content you enter to generate QR codes is not sent to our servers by the app. If you share, export, or save a QR code using another app or service, that external app or service may process the data according to its own privacy policy.

4. Saved QR Codes and Local Data

If the app includes a history, favorites, templates, or saved QR codes feature, that information is stored locally on your device so you can access it later. You can delete this information from within the app, if such option is available, or by clearing the app data/uninstalling the app from your device settings.

5. Device Storage

If you choose to save a generated QR code as an image, the app may use your device storage or media permissions, depending on your Android version and system settings. These permissions are used only to save or access QR code files that you choose to create or use.

6. Third-Party Services

QR Generate may use third-party services provided by Google Play or the Android operating system to distribute, maintain, or improve the app. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

If advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or similar third-party SDKs are added to the app in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe those services and the information they may process.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share the content used to create QR codes with third parties, except when you choose to share, export, save, or otherwise use that content through another app or service.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable measures to keep the app safe and to limit data processing to what is necessary for the app to function. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Children’s Privacy

QR Generate is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

10. Your Choices

  • You decide what information to enter when generating a QR code.
  • You can delete locally stored app data through your device settings.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.

12. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the app listing or the official support channel provided in Google Play.
