Privacy Policy for Feed Reader

Last updated: May 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Feed Reader handles information when you use the app. Feed Reader is designed to let you subscribe to and read public RSS, Atom, and other feed formats while minimizing data collection.

1. Information We Collect

Feed Reader does not require account registration and does not ask for personal information such as your name, phone number, or home address.

The app may process feed-related data necessary to operate, including feed URLs you add, article titles, summaries, publication dates, and links provided by feed publishers.

2. How Information Is Used

Feed data is used only to fetch, parse, organize, and display content inside the app. We do not use your reading activity for profiling or unrelated advertising.

3. Local Storage

Feed subscriptions, cached articles, and app preferences are stored locally on your device to improve performance and offline access. By default, this data is not uploaded to developer-controlled servers.

4. Network Access

Feed Reader uses internet access to retrieve content from feed sources you choose. When a request is made, your device may share technical information (such as IP address and user-agent) with the feed host, as part of normal web communication.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. Data is shared with third parties only when required to deliver app functionality (for example, requesting content from feed providers) or when you explicitly choose to share links or content through other apps.

6. Third-Party Services

Feed Reader may rely on platform services such as Google Play for distribution and updates. These services may process limited technical information under their own privacy policies.

If analytics, crash reporting, cloud sync, or advertising tools are added in future versions, this policy will be updated to describe those changes.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Stored feed data remains on your device until you remove subscriptions, clear cache, clear app data, or uninstall the app.

8. Security

We apply reasonable safeguards to protect stored information. However, no method of storage or internet transmission is completely secure.

9. Children’s Privacy

Feed Reader is not directed to children and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your Choices

  • You can remove any feed subscription at any time.
  • You can clear cached content and app data from device settings.
  • You can uninstall Feed Reader at any time.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

12. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the support channel listed in the app store listing.
