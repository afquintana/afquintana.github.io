Privacy Policy for Task Tick

Last updated: May 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Task Tick handles information when you use the app. Task Tick is designed to help you create, manage, and complete tasks while keeping your data usage transparent and limited to app functionality.

1. Information We Collect

Task Tick does not require account registration and does not request personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, or home address.

The app may process information you create inside the app, such as task titles, descriptions, due dates, completion status, reminders, and preference settings.

2. Permissions

Task Tick may request limited device permissions only when required for specific features, such as notifications for reminders. Permissions are optional unless they are needed for a feature you choose to use.

You can manage or revoke permissions at any time through your device settings.

3. Local Data Storage

Task data is stored locally on your device by default. We do not upload your tasks to developer-controlled servers unless a future cloud backup or sync option is clearly presented and explicitly enabled by you.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. Your task content is not shared with third parties except when you explicitly choose to export or share it using other apps or services available on your device.

5. Third-Party Services

Task Tick may rely on platform services (such as Google Play and Android system services) for distribution, updates, and basic reliability. Those services may process limited technical information under their own privacy policies.

If analytics, crash reporting, cloud sync, or advertising SDKs are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated to describe what data is processed and for what purpose.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete tasks, clear app data, or uninstall the app. If cleanup tools are provided in the app, you can use them to remove selected entries or all stored content.

7. Security

We apply reasonable safeguards designed to reduce unauthorized access and protect data handled by the app. However, no storage method can be guaranteed to be fully secure.

8. Children’s Privacy

Task Tick is not directed to children and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your Choices

  • You can edit or delete tasks at any time.
  • You can disable optional permissions, such as notifications, in device settings.
  • You can clear app data or uninstall Task Tick whenever you choose.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the support channel listed in the app store listing.
