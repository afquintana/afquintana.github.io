Privacy Policy for Instrument Tuner

Last updated: May 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Instrument Tuner handles information when you use the app. Instrument Tuner is designed to help users tune musical instruments while minimizing data collection and prioritizing on-device processing.

1. Information We Collect

Instrument Tuner does not require account registration and does not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or address to use the core tuning features.

The app may process audio input from your device microphone to detect pitch and provide tuning guidance in real time. This audio is used only for the tuning function.

2. Microphone Permission

Instrument Tuner requires microphone access to analyze sound frequencies from your instrument. Without microphone permission, the app cannot perform tuning.

Microphone input is not used to identify you and is not collected for profiling purposes.

3. Local Processing

Audio analysis for pitch detection is intended to occur locally on your device. Instrument Tuner does not upload raw microphone audio to our servers for tuning operations.

4. Stored Preferences and Local Data

The app may store settings locally on your device, such as selected tuning standard (for example, A4 frequency), preferred theme, instrument type presets, or calibration options. This data helps personalize your experience and remains on your device.

5. Third-Party Services

Instrument Tuner may rely on platform services (for example, Google Play and Android system components) to distribute and maintain the app. These services may process data under their own privacy policies.

If analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or other third-party SDKs are integrated in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe what information is processed and for what purpose.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share microphone audio captured for tuning with third parties.

7. Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the app and limit data processing to what is necessary for functionality. However, no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

8. Children’s Privacy

Instrument Tuner is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact the developer so appropriate action can be taken.

9. Your Choices

  • You can deny microphone permission, although tuning features will not work.
  • You can clear local app data through your device settings.
  • You can uninstall the app at any time.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page and the “Last updated” date will be revised accordingly.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the app listing or the official support channel provided in Google Play.
