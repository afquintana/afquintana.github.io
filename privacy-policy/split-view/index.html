Privacy Policy

Split View
Last updated: May 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Split View accesses, uses, protects, and shares information related to users.

App Owner

The app is operated by Antonio Quintana. For any questions about this Privacy Policy or data handling, you can contact the developer at: mail@afquintana.com.

Data Access and Permissions

Split View is designed to help users work with multi-window and split-screen related features on their devices. The app only accesses data and permissions that are needed for these core features.

  • The app does not sell personal data.
  • The app does not access personal files, photos, or contacts unless explicitly stated in-app.
  • The app does not upload user content to the developer's servers.

Automatically Collected Data

The app uses Firebase services provided by Google to improve stability and understand general app usage:

  • Firebase Crashlytics: collects crash reports, technical device information, app version, crash logs, and related diagnostic data needed to identify and fix problems.
  • Firebase Analytics: collects usage events and aggregated technical information, such as device information, operating system, approximate country, app interactions, and app instance identifiers.

How Data Is Used

Collected technical data is used to:

  • Maintain app reliability and performance.
  • Detect and fix crashes and errors.
  • Understand feature usage to improve the app experience.

Data Sharing with Third Parties

Split View does not sell user data. Technical data collected through Firebase may be processed by Google as a service provider under its applicable privacy terms.

Data Retention

The developer does not store personal user content on private servers. Diagnostic and analytics data collected by Firebase is retained according to Google and Firebase retention settings and policies.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information handled by the app and connected services.

Children's Privacy

Split View is not specifically directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

User Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, object to, or restrict processing of personal data. To exercise these rights, contact: mail@afquintana.com.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in app functionality, legal requirements, or services used. The latest version date is shown at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: mail@afquintana.com.
